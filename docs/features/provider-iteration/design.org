* Abstract injection and complete-join iteration (2026-10-07)

=ProviderIteration= connects the positive-body delta law to abstract head
injection and complete program histories. =CompleteJoin= declares reflexive,
transitive, antisymmetric order and arbitrary-family supremum upper/least laws.
An abstract database may be a product of heterogeneous relation domains; a head
fact is injected into the appropriate coordinate by =head=. The module does
not assume that the database itself is a powerset or that concretization
preserves joins. These semantic obligations are not inferred from a Scheme
procedure or certified by a shape check.

=inject= is the supremum of the tuple injections admitted by a rule-head family.
=inject_mono= and =inject_union= derive monotone accumulation and union/join
correspondence. =heads_partition= lifts ProviderAdmission's arbitrary finite
body partition through rule projection. =pivot_after_full= then derives that
all-old head injections are already subsumed by the retained abstract state.
=iterations_equal= proves equality of naive and semi-naive abstract histories
for every natural-number round, including empty bodies and repeated relations.
Delta may include old facts; it must be sound and cover newly concretized facts.
Non-pivot occurrences read the merged concrete snapshot. This is the library's
synchronous merged-snapshot strategy, not a proof of every scheduler or the
paper's exact operational presentation of the split total/delta pair.

The complete-join laws also construct the least closed state above an arbitrary
initial state. =least_fixed= establishes its fixed-point equation without an
assumed completed run. =stationary_eq_least= identifies any stabilized history
with that state. =ranked_converges= connects parameterized bounded rank and strict
progress to finite convergence of both execution histories to the same least
state. Neither a fixed generation cap nor finite convergence of arbitrary
infinite-height domains is asserted. A complete lattice provides fixed-point
existence; finite execution needs the separate rank/progress witness.

Tests instantiate a two-element abstract lattice that collapses distinct head
facts to one state. Injection can expose implied observations absent from the
raw input tuple family. Controls reject anti-monotone concretization and show
that an empty fresh head state cannot overwrite an accumulated true state.
Axioms and tests belong under =packages/proofs/lean/Tests=.

The existing ProviderFrontier Quint model adds a =replace= fault that overwrites
accumulated consumer heads with only the current pivot output. Its independent
=ConsumerSnapshot= invariant must reject the fault. This is a publication and
retention control, not a second proof of abstract join algebra. Native retained
sessions check eqrel, trrel and trrel_uf with a three-occurrence downstream body,
a delayed new edge, an already-implied new source tuple and repeated runs.
Independent graph closure and explicit body witnesses determine the expected
heads; earlier published results must remain unchanged.

This closes the conditional abstract injection, round-equivalence, least-state
and ranked-convergence components. Universal native state/key/equality
extraction, asynchronous custom-domain iteration, general lattice projection
semantics and paper application/cost acceptance retain their separate gates.
See [[file:../../evidence/scheme-provider-views-20261007/abstract-provider-iteration.json][the abstract iteration receipt]].

Qualification passed 97 Lean build jobs, 25 positive TLC configurations and 65
fault controls. The native BYODS invariants suite passed 14 cases with final
=OK=, including all three retained-injection Provider controls. The receipt
retains the actual five-state overwrite counterexample and source SHA256s;
raw execution transcripts are excluded from Git history.
