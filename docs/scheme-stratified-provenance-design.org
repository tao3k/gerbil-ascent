* Complete grounded equations

=candidate/stratified-provenance.ss= enumerates every successful grounded rule
instance over an independently verified complete finite closure. Source leaves
retain occurrence positions, so duplicate input occurrences remain distinct.
Fact nodes are relation-tagged and may have several alternatives. Recursive
references, including self-cycles, remain finite equations rather than being
expanded into an infinite list of trees.

Each positive atom records its fact-node reference. A negative clause records
its grounded pattern, preserving wildcards, and is justified by the exact
completed lower-stratum model. A reduction records the entire distinct matched
group, including an empty group for count and sum. Empty min/max groups produce
no alternative. Fixed computations and filters use inspected closed operators.

The checker independently rebuilds all nodes, alternatives and query roots and
compares their source generation, snapshot digest and inspected program binding.
Missing alternatives, extra alternatives, mutated roots, caller-owned cycles,
noncomplete native results and resource exhaustion cannot verify as complete.
An empty query can have a complete graph with no roots; this is not a general
Why-Not or source-authentication claim.

* Scope and representation

This is complete derivation provenance for the admitted finite stratified rule
language, with recursive positive components and exact negative/reduction
observations. It describes a frozen completed model. Counterfactual deletion
must update that model; absence and reduction observations cannot be reused
blindly under changed source facts. Native Session invalidation handles that
update boundary. Numerical valuations in other semirings and lattice-join
histories require their own algebra and observation contract.

Qualification includes independently counted alternatives on all 64 directed
three-node graphs, duplicate sources, recursive cycles, wildcard negation,
empty queries/groups, exact reductions, missing-edge and cycle tampering,
source-generation changes and independent resource caps. Runtime deadlines,
heap fences and genuine progress requirements remain unchanged.
