#+TITLE: ASCENT feature design, implementation and assurance

* Navigation and ownership

Each feature groups its design, paper correspondence, semantic proofs, protocol
models, native implementation and acceptance evidence. Lean owns semantic and
algebraic laws; TLA+ owns lifecycle and interleaving obligations. Historical
receipts retain their source identity and do not certify a later commit.

Update the relevant feature page when advancing its implementation. Keep
cross-feature scope and ordering in the [[file:../scheme-paper-closure.org][closure matrix]] and
[[file:../scheme-research-proof-audit.org][research audit]] overviews. Preserve explicit remaining obligations.
