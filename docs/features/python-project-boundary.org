engine. Scheme retains semantic ownership and its native =t/= tests.

archived producers keep their original bytes/SHA; an active producer move does
not refresh a past source identity or transfer a paid-call authorization.

* Python orchestration and finite checking

The formal entry calls one Python workbench command. The former Shell checker
is removed. Declarative model configurations preserve the 51 models and all 327
original checks; four arithmetic counterexamples additionally reject negative
counters and future completion snapshots. Native CPU/heap capacity schedules
independent TLC processes. A fresh process remains mandatory for every native
consumer file/backend because runtime shutdown is terminal.

The failed GitHub formal job reached hundreds of millions of generated states
and was cancelled at its original 15-minute deadline. Two new models had
unbounded cost/event counters. Increasing parallelism cannot make an infinite
state space exhaustible.

=AccountingAbstraction.lean= proves that every nonnegative increment is included
in the finite sign/order transitions, and that the observed safety predicates
have the same truth values. Negative counters and snapshots after current cost
remain represented. Arbitrarily many actions still run: no call-count cutoff or
generation-bound reduction was introduced. These laws concern the modeled
safety observations; they do not prove arbitrary Scheme semantics or liveness.

* Native publication permission

An =EPERM= hard-link denial now fails publication explicitly. It cannot silently
publish a copied compiled generation. The original cross-volume copy behavior
remains recorded as =copied=; it does not claim retained-inode performance.
Mutable metadata continues to be copied. Exact generation names, byte checks
and removal of unrecorded contiguous generations retain their existing contract.

Earlier copied diagnostic packages timed out during official dynamic loading
and Darwin signature checks. A retained-generation diagnostic passed the six
lifecycle cases in 8.737/15 seconds; the subsequent no-diagnostic package passed
in 9.562/15 seconds. This does not establish portable cold-load performance or
stable Actor execution acceleration. Historical failures are preserved.
