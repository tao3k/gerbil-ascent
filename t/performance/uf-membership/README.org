#+title: Frozen weighted membership and complete delta export

* Architecture
The existing immutable rectangle routing index also answers membership queries.
A value hit must name the exact captured member owner; globally present left
and right values cannot be combined across unrelated rectangles. Mixed schemas
and nonindexed views keep checked member scans. Very short axes retain bounded
direct scans. Point queries allocate no reader index or descriptor callback.

Difference export uses this membership protocol only when the older cut carries
the private final =rectangle-visitors= specialization. Generic/custom visitors
retain their checked/admitted full-row probe, including cases whose =contains=
callback disagrees with their parts. Public tuples, outer spines, order, counts
and historical cuts retain their existing ownership contracts. No tuple cache,
new environment setting or public structure change is introduced.

The design follows Gerbil's native =std/cache.ss= final carrier specialization
and =std/list/list-builder.ss= invocation ownership. Pure owner/descriptor scans
are named tail-recursive functions; row callbacks are absent from point probes.
