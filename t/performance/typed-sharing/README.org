* Workloads and fixed gates

Descriptors are prepared outside timing. Every timed call performs full typed
compilation, complete Program validation and ordered projection of all declared
arities, rows, finite domains and rule count. Both sides compile native; public
admit/solve/query independently checks identity-set truth before sampling.

- source: domain 512, 512 rows, five shared union layers.
- mapping: identity mapping over that source, four shared union layers.
- single: domain 512, eight rows, no shared layer.
- small: domain two, two rows, one shared layer.

All compiler budgets are input=32768, derived=32768, output=32768 and
expansion=10000. These limits retain mapping source occurrence multiplicity.
Native ASP =benchmark-run/result= admits 20 optimized complete consumers;
20 alternating CPU pairs include natural GC. Five separate allocation pairs
use monotone cumulative VM statistic 16 with forced collections. Every result
matches the frozen ordered publication projection. CPU target gates require
<=90% baseline and >=16/20 wins. Controls allow <=110% plus 20 microseconds;
allocation allows <=110% plus 4096 bytes. Gates were fixed before sampling.

the accepted series; its earlier source measurement remains separate.

* Allocation counter qualification boundary (2026-10-10)

The historical allocation pairs above sampled process-statistics slot 16 at
forced-GC boundaries. The lattice-frontier study observed negative differences
in a fully static executable, both with that recipe and with Gambit's dedicated
=##get-bytes-allocated!= counter. Historical byte counts and allocation gates
are therefore withdrawn pending a corrected gxi receipt. Historical CPU and
semantic receipts remain separate evidence. The runner now samples the dedicated
cumulative counter directly around the complete consumer and rejects a negative
difference. This change does not inherit the preceding allocation PASS.
