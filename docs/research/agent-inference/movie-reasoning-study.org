#+TITLE: Complex movie reasoning: execution assistance and reusable subgoals
#+DATE: 2026-10-10
#+STATUS: real multi-hop pilot and adaptive diagnostics; held-out main evaluation pending

* Research question and present boundary

Does ASCENT reduce the total model reasoning cost of complex knowledge-graph
questions while preserving answer quality and scope-correct evidence? Does
reusing admitted subgoals add a benefit beyond deterministic execution alone?

The current six movie questions are mechanism regressions. They exercise
contract admission, typed film/actor joins, scoped exclusions and changed
bindings on 90 pinned P161 claims from three films. Their deliberately chosen
six baseline versus three contract calls do not measure model ability,
reasoning-token savings or monetary savings. The 1,536-state correspondence
check qualifies a finite executor abstraction, not natural-language understanding.
The completed real pilot executed nine provider calls without retries. Direct
answers were correct on 5/6 questions; scoped-union transfer was incomplete
after consuming its 4,096-token output limit entirely on reasoning. Three
contract calls produced correct Scheme answers on all six question bindings.
Direct calls consumed 12,241 output tokens (11,432 reasoning); contract calls
consumed 335 (293 reasoning). These are observed pilot totals under a deliberately
constructed reuse schedule, not an estimate of general savings or model ability.
Offline gold remains a control, never a model prediction. Raw responses, usage
and Scheme grades are retained under =.cache/ascent/movie-study/real-20261010=.

[[https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/miao26e.html][LLM-MatLogic]] section 4.1 describes filtering simple subgraphs for its
main experiment, matching query-pattern sampling and sharing retrieved subgraphs
between methods. Its minimal movie example is useful for a scope regression;
it is not an adequate main benchmark for a modern strong model.

* Complex task construction

Complexity must come from dependencies, not prompt length or extra distractors.
Cover path depth, joins sharing an actor variable across distinct film anchors,
union inside conjunction, scope-local negation before projection, relation
direction, and changes to facts supporting a previously reusable result.
Keep simple questions as a separate calibration stratum.

An illustrative target is: actors shared by films whose directors satisfy an
award constraint and films whose production companies satisfy a country
constraint, with a release-date constraint and a cameo exclusion confined to
the first branch. Returning an actor requires both branch witnesses, the
correct date/role interpretation and the appropriate exclusion scope.
This is a task specification, not a claim that the current P161 fixture or
two-field contract can express it. Director, award, company, country, date and
role-qualified cast evidence must first be acquired, licensed, pinned and
represented. Missing role qualifiers cannot be interpreted as non-cameo facts.
Date comparisons and additional operators require Scheme implementation and
qualification before admitting those task strata.

Use both independently annotated natural-language questions and structured
paper-pattern queries. Freeze gold typed query structure, scoped witnesses
and answer sets separately from provider inputs; audit annotations before
execution. Split by query structure and entity anchors, rather than only
rewriting names. Include paraphrases that change scope and plausible hard
negatives, empty answers, competing witnesses and multiple correct answers.
Reserve held-out structures to test generalization rather than template recall.

* Three matched experimental arms

| Arm | Model and Scheme work | Comparison |
|-----+-----------------------+------------|
| Direct model reasoning | Strong model solves each question using the frozen evidence and an equal retrieval entitlement; capture all tool/model work. | Reference quality and cost. |
| Contract execution without reuse | Same model specifies each question independently; Scheme admits and executes each contract. | Execution assistance versus direct model reasoning. |
| Contract execution with subgoal reuse | Same admission and execution, with qualifying subgoals reused across questions. | Incremental reuse benefit versus execution without reuse. |

Do not weaken the reference prompt or withhold evidence/tools to create a win.
Give all arms the same source boundary, question, model version, reasoning
effort and allowed recovery policy. Count verification, rejected proposals,
repairs, retries and retrieval calls in the arm that incurred them. A gold
contract execution is a diagnostic upper bound, excluded from model accuracy.

* Workload and reuse admission

Report cold queries, warm queries sharing actual subgoals, and queries following
source updates separately. Freeze workload order and reuse prevalence before
running; do not choose a fixed repetition ratio to manufacture a saving.
Show the cost curve as reuse prevalence varies, including zero reuse.

Updates include insertion of a new answer witness, withdrawal of a supporting
fact, changed scope/role constraints, and changes unrelated to a cached subgoal.
Measure invalidation, revalidation and recomputation, including their Scheme
cost. Compare every reuse result with fresh execution on the current snapshot.
Never return an old answer solely because a template or prompt string matches.

Reuse admission needs typed query structure, bindings, exclusion scope,
source identity and evidence completeness. Equivalence of natural-language
intent cannot be assumed from superficial wording. A reuse decision and its
current witnesses must be inspectable. The current movie fixture establishes
only caller-declared template reuse with answer recomputation, not this general
subgoal mechanism.

* Measurements and decision rule

Primary quality measures are exact answer-set agreement and scope-correct
witness validity. Diagnose interpretation/admission/execution errors separately.
Keep empty, failed, rejected and timed-out questions in the denominator.

Primary economic measures are total billed tokens and priced provider cost,
including planning, verification and recovery. Record requested and resolved
model, cached/uncached input, output and reasoning tokens when exposed, provider
usage, wall time and Scheme execution cost. Reasoning-token savings cannot be
claimed if reasoning usage is unavailable; missing usage does not become zero.
Calls are a secondary measure. Provider prefix caching and ASCENT semantic reuse
must be accounted for separately.

Use matched question pairs, counterbalanced arm order and repeated trials.
Group uncertainty estimates by query family/source episode so repeated variants
are not treated as independent samples. Freeze the quality tolerance, workload,
analysis and trial budget before live execution; determine sample size using
pilot variance. Do not promise statistical power from an arbitrary case count.
The 40–50% reduction is a hypothesis for the complex workload. A claim requires
measured total cost reduction together with the preregistered quality gate;
quality degradation or expensive repairs are retained as negative results.

* Scheme, Lean and Quint responsibilities

Scheme owns actual admission, query execution, invalidation and witness receipts.
Lean establishes the semantic conditions for scope preservation, shared-variable
joins and valid reuse. Quint explores update sequences and stale-result faults.
Counterexamples must become matching Scheme regressions. Foreign-model passes
alone cannot qualify a production implementation or a model answer.

The existing finite movie correspondence is a foundation for those obligations.
It does not prove arbitrary complex query compilation, general reuse, or any
empirical model-cost result. Expand each admitted operator across all three
artifacts before using it in the main workload.

* Scheme transport and cold startup

The pilot used one temporary AOT Scheme batch scorer to diagnose repeated
module-import cost. This is an experimental receipt, not a supported public
interface or a scene-specific build command. Python keeps provider byte IO;
Scheme constructs truth, admits contracts and grades actual answers. The frozen plan binds the
executable and every versioned =.oN= object and compiler metadata, not only the
potentially stale =.o1= file. Producer source hashes and runtime artifact hashes
are identities, not a general proof of source-to-binary refinement.

The implemented integration is a general ASCENT C ABI, implemented on the Scheme
side through its Scheme FFI, and a Python CFFI binding. Python owns model
transport and connects directly to Scheme engine operations; the engine owns
query admission, reusable sessions, updates, execution and witness receipts.
The interface must specify runtime/session initialization, handle and buffer
ownership, thread affinity, error/status propagation and cancellation. A model
scenario consumes this interface; it does not introduce a separate Scheme build.
Avoid arbitrary model-generated Python/Scheme evaluation.

The public interface is =bindings/c/ascent.h=, =interface/engine-api.ss= and
=python/src/ascent_engine=. The normal Gerbil package build compiles the Scheme
modules; the Python package links their compiler-derived closure into one
engine shared library. There is no movie-specific Scheme build command.
The supported wire subset is finite typed relations, literals, variables,
positive atoms and stratified negation, with explicit update-source authority.
Arbitrary host predicates, aggregates and procedure callbacks are unsupported.
Actual CFFI qualification replayed all 1,536 frozen Lean/Quint observations,
and checked UTF-8, duplicate keys, transactional rejection, thread affinity,
stale handles and terminal runtime shutdown. This does not prove C memory safety
or refinement for arbitrary programs. Measure runtime initialization, module loading, FFI marshaling,
request time and Scheme execution separately. Reusing the initialized runtime
and sessions removes repeated local setup; remote inference latency remains a
separate measurement.

* Execution gates

1. Qualify the current small fixture as a mechanism regression only.
2. Acquire and independently annotate complex frozen evidence and query strata.
3. Implement missing typed contracts/operators and subgoal reuse, with Scheme
   regressions, Lean obligations and Quint update/fault controls.
4. Freeze matched requests and offline oracle/witness checks without model calls.
5. Run an authorized pilot, choose the main sample size, preregister the quality
   tolerance and cost analysis, then run the held-out evaluation.

The user authorized real model testing on 2026-10-10 and supplied the project
=.env= location. The key was read as data without shell execution or printing.
The pilot was bounded to nine calls and one dollar without automatic retries.
The first three charged requests used the frozen temporary AOT scorer. The
remaining six used a frozen copy of the public CFFI library; all prior responses
were regraded through CFFI with matching results and no repeated model calls.
The explicit backend transition is recorded in =ffi-cut/transition.json=;
=ffi-result.json= retains all nine outcomes, including the incomplete response.
The compact tracked receipt is [[file:movie-pilot-result.json][movie-pilot-result.json]];
it records individual usage, failures, Scheme grades and response hashes.
The continuation took 22.757 seconds on this host, including remote calls and
Scheme grading. Complex workload construction
and qualification remain the preceding gates above. The six-question fixture
remains a mechanism regression even with real model responses. The separate
source-closure study's 60-call budget is not the movie study's budget.

** 完整题目与事实边界

本次失败诊断对应的完整题目是：

#+begin_quote
仅使用给定的有限 P161 演员事实：找出演过《盗梦空间》且不是莱昂纳多·迪卡普里奥的演员，或演过《荒野猎人》的演员。排除条件只作用于《盗梦空间》分支；最终结果按演员去重，返回演员 QID。不得引入资料之外的事实。
#+end_quote

原始英文模板：
=Actors in $left except $blocked, OR actors in $right; the exclusion applies only to the left branch.=
绑定为 left=Q25188（Inception）、right=Q18002795（The Revenant）、
blocked=Q38111（Leonardo DiCaprio）。语义是
=(Cast(Inception) \ {Leonardo}) ∪ Cast(The Revenant)=。
莱昂纳多必须通过右分支保留；将排除条件提升到整个并集会给出错误答案。

冻结事实包含 90 条 P161 记录：盗梦空间 21、荒野猎人 18、泰坦尼克号 51。
泰坦尼克号在本题中是无关事实。事实完整性仅针对冻结有限提取，
不声称覆盖现实世界全部演员。此题涉及两个分支、局部否定、并集、去重和证据；
它属于机制回归，不能充当复杂多跳能力主实验。

** 实验计划与目标

原始九次调用在请求、来源和评分器冻结后执行：六次直接回答，三次契约编译；
每个契约由 Scheme 对种子与迁移绑定分别执行。随后针对唯一未完成响应，
追加两次诊断（模型反思及约束更明确的直接回答），再追加一次仅提高输出上限的对照。
共十二次真实调用，无自动重试。后续诊断具有适应性，不能作为独立留出集。

目标是区分传输失败、生成未完成和答案错误，验证模型提出的作用域契约能否在
实际 Scheme 引擎正确执行，并检查提高输出上限后的原题结果。
评分使用预先冻结的私有答案与实际引擎，提示不携带私有答案。
仅提高上限的对照保持原题、事实、模型别名、high reasoning 和其余请求字段不变。

* 复杂多跳电影试验：公平三组对照与来源撤回

** 研究来源与具体问题

[[https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/miao26e.html][LLM-MatLogic]] 区分自然语言规格错误与执行错误，并强调在投影丢失证据之前实施作用域排除。
本试验将这些设计点落实到已有 Scheme 类型关系、分层否定和物化视图接口；
额外分离执行辅助与子目标复用的贡献。它不复现论文全部基准或其结果。

完整首题：
#+begin_quote
仅使用冻结事实。L：演员出演某部电影，该电影导演具有英国国籍，且电影不是《盗梦空间》。
R：演员出演某部电影，该电影的演员列表也包含莱昂纳多；演员可以就是莱昂纳多本人。
找出同时满足 L 和 R 的演员，只在 L 排除莱昂纳多。L 与 R 可以由不同电影支持，
必须连接同一演员。按演员 QID 去重。事实缺失只表示在本有限提取中不存在。
#+end_quote

L 需要 cast → directed → citizen 三关系连接；R 需要通过同一电影连接两条 cast 事实。
两组最后通过同一 Actor 连接。国籍属于导演，不能误施加到演员；两组不能强迫使用同一电影。
影片集合为《盗梦空间》《星际穿越》《黑暗骑士》《荒野猎人》《敦刻尔克》《泰坦尼克号》。
来源包含 165 条 cast、6 条 directed、6 条 citizen，附实体 revision、原始 JSON 哈希、
statement ID、rank 与 qualifier。只提取有值且非 deprecated 的 P161/P57/P27；
资格元数据保留但不解释为客串或日期语义。事实是在本试验中冻结的有限提取，非完整世界知识。

** 对照、预算与评分

八题分别覆盖 AND/OR × 左分支排除/两分支排除/无排除，以及两次事实撤回。
每题三组：模型直接回答、模型生成一般关系规则后 Scheme 执行、提供 Scheme 实际物化子目标后执行。
组顺序轮换，每组八次调用，共 24 次，无自动重试。模型别名 deepseek-flash、high reasoning、
max_output_tokens=65536。三组具有相同原始事实、题目、实体域和数据边界；复用组额外获得
330 条 eligible 与 90 条 coactor 物化记录。复用记录是处理条件，不是题目金标准。
其额外输入成本必须计入；当前通过枚举传递视图，可能造成负收益。
冻结计划最坏输出费用估算约 2.0101 美元，不是账户余额或项目总预算。

金标准在真实调用前由冻结 Scheme 查询构造，单独保存于计划，不进入提示。
主要评分是答案集合完全一致；本试验不是独立自然语言标注的大规模留出评估。
一般规则接口允许模型组合关系和变量，不再局限于旧电影契约的两个枚举字段。
模型不能修改来源声明或向来源关系/已有物化关系写入规则头。

** 三位一体对应与故障控制

Lean MaterializedMovieQuery 证明：当前两组物化关系分别与其关系语义逐点等价时，
替换后的答案语义相同。这一条件不能用旧来源的身份或旧视图代替。
Quint 独立计算 512 个有限来源快照与六种作用域/连接条件，共 3072 个观察。
Lean 对应输出与当前 Quint 输出、冻结语料一致；实际 Scheme CFFI 对全部观察进行重放。
资格检查使用两个保留会话实施来源与视图替换，避免为每个观察反复创建和编译查询。
本地主机上此检查约 1.45 秒；单次用时不代表稳定性能提升。

另外故意注入三类故障：将局部排除提升为全局排除、将 AND 错换为 OR、来源撤回后继续使用旧视图。
三类均由实际 Scheme 对照检出。它们是故障控制，不是声称当前生产实现存在这三个 BUG。
对应证明、语料和 Scheme 检查已接入现有 formal 与 CFFI 资格测试入口。

** 结论边界与结果记录

本轮是较复杂的多跳可行性试验，仍只有八个高度相关的问题、一次重复和六部电影。
不能用它给出总体准确率增益、置信区间、40%–50% 节省或独立结构泛化结论。
保留失败和负收益；原始评分不被后续结构修复或诊断重写。
单元素规则头结构修复只将 head 对象包为单元素数组，不改变规则正文、变量、常量和算子。
修复后另行检查原快照及两种撤回；这些是零额外模型调用的事后诊断。
下一阶段应改进契约结构约束和物化视图传递，再冻结独立结构、实体和来源的留出集。

两个结构失败都是 head 被写成对象而非数组；另一次模型将 OR 写为 AND，实际返回 3 人而非 154 人。

输出约束的原题对照通过，输出 4034 token。新编译请求已默认使用该结构约束。
针对语义失败，模型在未获得金标准答案的情况下检查原题和自己的程序，
指出同一规则体是 AND、分支规则才是 OR；修正契约由 Scheme 执行得到 154 人。
三个诊断调用合计估算 0.027281 美元，不能从效果或成本统计中隐藏。
JSON Schema 只约束结构，不能保证自然语言语义；新回归包含实际 OR/AND 反例。

首轮还有选样缺口：三个 OR 变体几乎输出整个 155 人实体域。

* Retained inference: structural holdouts and repeated controls

** 实验目标与冻结边界

本轮分开测量首次生成查询、完全相同任务再次执行、事实撤回后复用查询逻辑。
前一轮 reuse 仅缓存物化视图，每道题仍调用模型，14.3% 不代表第二次路径复用。
本轮直接使用 CFFI Session：首次 open/run、重复 run(0)、来源变化 replace/run。
Python 负责实验编排和 provider IO，关系推理及来源更新由 Scheme 执行。
首次指首次生成契约，不是 OS 进程或模型服务冷启动；API 估算不包含机器费用。

四种此前电影试验未测的结构，各用独立命名的合成实体。
每个来源包含 12 个 Actor、6 个 Film、3 个 Director、2 个 Country，不是新真实 Wikidata 数据。
每种结构两轮独立模型生成，首次调用先后顺序轮换，每轮三个执行阶段。
直接回答每阶段调用模型；紧凑接口仅首次调用，之后保留相同程序会话。
原题与事实不变时通用答案缓存也能零调用，另外报告这一公平基线。
来源变化后旧答案不能使用，已有程序在新来源上执行并生成新答案。

冻结计划后调用模型。错误和输出预算耗尽保留，主实验不自动修复或重试。
首次契约失败使其后两个复用阶段也失败，不能把不可执行路径计为免费正确答案。
两轮重复用于发现波动，不足以证明统计稳定性；合成独立实体不覆盖真实世界分布。
编译器只有自然语言、关系类型和含义，没有事实、实体域或金标准。
直接回答获得完整同源事实，Scheme 对源数据的拥有是本实验处理条件。

** 完整题目与预先核对的事实变化

*** mentor-chain

Return actors x with a directed mentor edge x->y and y->z, where z acts in a film whose director has the selected_country. The two mentor edges must share y; cast, directed and citizen must share the same film and director. Actors may repeat along the path; do not require x itself to act. Use only the supplied finite relations. Return unique actor identifiers.

撤回：{'relation': 'mentor', 'row': ['holdout0_actor11', 'holdout0_actor0']}。独立集合 oracle 与 Scheme 金标准已核对；撤回前 10 个答案，撤回后 8 个答案。

*** witness-local-exclusion

Return actors with at least one film directed by a director with selected_country, with that supporting film not in banned_film. Exclude individual supporting films before projecting actor: acting in a banned film must not exclude an actor who also has another qualifying, unbanned film. Use only the supplied finite relations. Return unique actor identifiers.

撤回：{'relation': 'cast', 'row': ['holdout1_film2', 'holdout1_actor2']}。独立集合 oracle 与 Scheme 金标准已核对；撤回前 6 个答案，撤回后 5 个答案。

*** anti-existential

Return actors in actor_domain with at least one film whose director has selected_country AND with no appearance in any banned_film. The second condition excludes the entire actor if ANY banned-film appearance exists, even if a different film qualifies. Use only the supplied finite relations. Return unique actor identifiers.

撤回：{'relation': 'cast', 'row': ['holdout2_film0', 'holdout2_actor2']}。独立集合 oracle 与 Scheme 金标准已核对；撤回前 5 个答案，撤回后 6 个答案。

*** recursive-mentor

Return actors that can reach an actor in target through ONE OR MORE directed mentor edges. Include any path length, including cycles; zero-length paths alone do not qualify. An actor in target qualifies only if it also has a positive-length path to target. Use only the supplied finite relations. Return unique actor identifiers.

撤回：{'relation': 'mentor', 'row': ['holdout3_actor0', 'holdout3_actor1']}。独立集合 oracle 与 Scheme 金标准已核对；撤回前 12 个答案，撤回后 11 个答案。

* Iterative Scheme evidence: 模型每轮推理中的证据增强

** 修正后的研究对象与两个验证

核心闭环是：模型提出要查证的子问题，Scheme 建立并接纳证据，
证据与来源身份进入下一轮，模型继续推理、补证或纠错，直到答案通过验证。
模型的 thinking 不等于外部可重复验证的证据链；Scheme 不读取或声称验证隐藏思维。
验证一：每题直到首次正确答案的累计模型轮次、reasoning token、全部模型 token 和检索/执行工作。
验证二：已验证同题意、同来源任务再次出现，直接执行已有路径，零模型调用。
验证二不能替代验证一；第一轮建立路径与所有失败/反思/修复开销必须纳入总账。

前面的单次 API 费用对照不是该增强机制的主指标；output_tokens 包含 reasoning，
但单次总量不能回答“几轮才找到正确答案”。新的 inference_effort 只累计到第一次通过验证，
失败和 query/scan 都计入，未成功的轨迹标为未完成。实际模型调用缺 usage 时为未知，不能算零。
可见答案的字节数不定义推理节省。原始事实相同但提供了执行工具，这是实验处理条件。

** 已落地的接口与来源失效

EvidenceStore 为真实 Scheme CFFI 会话提供原始关系扫描、规则执行、证据发布与复用。
完整求值后的 derived relation 发布为带 path/source 身份的只读证据别名。
第二轮规则可以直接引用第一轮别名，继续连接子目标，不必重新声明其规则。
模型不能改写原始事实、证据别名或向派生关系注入事实。
相同路径调用 run(0)，来源替换后旧派生别名与依赖消费者失效，基础查询路径更新后重新求值。
此功能已在真实 Scheme 上验证两段子目标组合、重复命中、撤回拒绝及重新建链。

EvidenceStore 的接纳保证是给定规则的有限来源推导，不是自然语言意图等价证明。
自然语言题意改变不能依靠相似度自动复用，当前实验也未实现广泛自然语言搜索匹配。
Lean 增加子目标关系等价条件下的扩展正确性；Quint 的有限语料与 Scheme 重放
另外检查 32 个已发布子目标扩展观察，不声称完整模型/引擎 refinement。

** 收紧处理条件的完整多轮对照

首轮控制组只能 scan，辅助组只能 query 并保留多列 witness；辅助答案必须已有完成证据。
每题最多四轮，两组可读取同一原始来源；verifier 仅反馈 verified/not_verified，不给金标准。
使用既有结构作为机制开发集，不冒充独立留出集。本轮 14 次模型调用、六条轨迹。

| 题目 | 控制组到首次正确轮数 | Scheme 组到首次正确轮数 | 控制 reasoning | Scheme reasoning |
|------+---------------------+------------------------+----------------+------------------|
| mentor-chain | 2 | 2 | 2798 | 3137 |
| anti-existential | 2 | 2 | 1912 | 4159 |
| recursive-mentor | 2 | 四轮未通过 | 1346 | 已消耗 10549 |

主实验成功率：控制组 3/3，Scheme 组 2/3。前两题 reasoning 分别增加 12.1% 和 117.5%。
不能给未完成的递归轨迹计算“到正确答案的节省比例”。本轮没有证明 40%–50% 目标。
更长证据与首次查询合成的开销可能抵消后续推理收益，不能预设 Scheme 每题必然省 token。

递归题规则可以成立，但把查询选择器写成 witness(X,Y,T)，实际 ABI 需要裸关系名 witness。
原错误反馈笼统，模型重复该格式；最终答案因无已接纳 Scheme 证据而被拒绝，处理条件未放松。
未来接口已增加关系名 pattern、明确协议文字、具体错误反馈与回归测试。
另做两轮适应性延续：先修正 query，Scheme 返回递归 witness，再由模型回答并通过验证。
累计原四轮与两轮恢复，共六轮，15260 reasoning token；原主实验仍是未完成，不改为四轮成功。
这次恢复闭合了接口缺口，但不是新留出成绩，也不是缩短推理的证据。

** 统一后续主线

从机制开发转向独立实体、独立结构组合与更大选择性差异的留出任务，冻结后不调参。
比较相同正确性门槛、相同轮次/恢复政策的完整轨迹；重复对照要计入首轮建链，
并分开报告成功率、首次正确轮数、累计 reasoning、检索/执行工作、稳态路径复用。
将已验证子目标的别名复用与较小的算子接口作为减少重复规则合成的实现方向；
跨轮子目标引用的实际 Scheme 回归已通过，其真实模型收益尚未单独验证。
每批准备前读取 reflection，把规格、传输、执行、检索缺口转成明确回归，
同时保留原始失败。预算不是当前瓶颈，任务难度、意图接纳与样本覆盖仍限制结论。

** 本轮交付验证

15 个定向测试通过：8 个轨迹/协议单元测试、6 个真实 Scheme 留出/撤回检查、1 个跨轮证据组合集成测试。
Lean/Quint 与实际 Scheme 的 32 观察对应、32 个子目标扩展及 8 个陈旧答案故障控制通过。
记录的是冻结 CFFI 库的本地验证，最新源码的 Ubuntu CI 另行验证；没有等待 macOS 作为主线门槛。
