* BYODS baseline and replication

The built-in Provider/frontier/shared-index mainline has Scheme implementations,
independent native/Rust controls, Lean laws and Quint protocols. Its current
engineering regression gates passed in revision =92475f6=, including all three
oracle jobs and Scheme performance/source/native checks
([[https://github.com/tao3k/gerbil-ascent/actions/runs/37915327136][completed CI]]). Preserve this accepted baseline rather than repeatedly treating
its mechanisms as absent implementation. Later revisions require their own CI.

Full Steensgaard input/partition correspondence is accepted. Complete upstream
Polonius reference observations and native complete-rule fixtures are accepted;
the full =clap-rs= native Scheme application comparison remains open. PageRank,
LLVM/index-sharing application replication, matched whole-application benefits
and universal native extraction remain distinct research work. The feature
[[file:../features/steensgaard/acceptance.org][Steensgaard]] and [[file:../features/polonius/acceptance.org][Polonius]] pages specify those source-bound limits.
