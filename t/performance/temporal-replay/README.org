#+title: Temporal binding and independent receipt replay

* Production flow and source learning

This study covers =temporal/lens.ss=: solve, verification, hypothetical fork
and generation comparison. The public POO extension boundary stays native.
An invocation-private final =temporal-binding= record names detached lens,
source, root, scope, cut and snapshot. =prepare-binding= owns cut admission
and content binding; =complete-receipt?= owns independent finite/founded
replay and native output limits; =answer-projection= owns the wire projection.

The installed Gerbil source at =1cfb032=, =compiler/optimize-base.ss:63-76=,
names the fields of its internal class metadata. Its
=compiler/optimize-call.ss:45-56= resolves applicability before selecting a
transformation. The local Coding Style representation guidance likewise
separates private records from observable wire lists. Here, a verified finite
certificate establishes exact closure and query rows; the projected cut and
coordinate digest establish applicability. Neither an object kind tag nor
an old answer's retained snapshot establishes that applicability.

Previously, verification called =solve= to build a second complete answer:
it ran the native engine, produced finite/founded certificates, replayed those
fresh certificates, then replayed the supplied certificates. Verification now
rebuilds the current cut and binding, independently replays the supplied
certificate, and compares the entire reconstructed projection. Fork and
comparison enter this same verifier before consuming accepted rows. Verification
uses the existing independent replay budgets; it no longer requires a fresh
producer to regenerate already valid supplied evidence. This is a certificate
verification boundary, not an assertion about producer capacity on every input.

The finite replay checks source/candidate input limits and derived limits.
Native output limits additionally include source rows: verification counts
all rows in the complete independently admitted certificate closure against
the fixed candidate's output limit. Empty query receipts still require exact
closure replay. Nonempty rows additionally require founded replay. Open,
rejected, bounded, tampered, relabelled and incompatible answers remain
inadmissible. No verdict cache, unchecked snapshot reuse or new environment
setting is introduced.

* Fixed complete-consumer A/B

=reference.ss= is byte-identical to
=797d6f4f489e03e4b5f4843f230eb2214df5174f:temporal/lens.ss=,
Git blob =f1d7024ce088bcb5d3001c9fc1656ade72b7193b=. Its dependencies are
shared with the production implementation. Fixture preparation creates inputs
and accepted answers outside timing. Each timed leg is the entire public
operation, including fresh cut projection, hashing, independent certificate
replay, projection detachment and compatibility checks. Fork also includes
its hypothetical solve and verification of the resulting answer.

Seven scenarios were fixed before sampling:

- verify: a ten-node chain, public verification.
- compare: the chain and a later empty generation, complete public delta.
- fork: the chain and a separately named empty hypothetical snapshot, followed
  by full verification of the hypothetical answer.
- empty: complete empty query, including finite closure verification.
- small: two nodes with one edge.
- partial: an open one-node cut, invalid verdict.
- solve: complete cold ten-node solve, guarding initial production behavior.

The runner directly calls ASP Scheme =benchmark-run/result=. It records
20 alternating CPU pairs, 20 independent ASP admissions and five alternating
allocation pairs via =##get-bytes-allocated!=. Natural collection remains in
CPU intervals; forced collection is outside them. All results must match the
frozen operation and independent verdict/delta/scope truth. Primary verify
and compare gates require CPU <=80% of baseline and >=16/20 wins; fork and
empty require <=90% and >=16/20 wins. Small, partial and solve require <=110% plus
20 microseconds. All allocations require <=110% plus 8192 bytes. Gates,
samples, work budgets and timeouts are not relaxed after measurement.

Cold solve passes its regression control, with 320 additional allocated bytes;
its 10/20 CPU wins do not establish a repeatable initial-solve speedup.
No general engine, actor or application throughput claim follows from these
specific composed consumers.

Formal raw receipts are =verify-final.ss= through =solve-final.ss= and
=all-final.log= in the ignored cache. Two earlier verify/compare diagnostics
ran while another task restarted a static compiler. They are preserved as
=verify-rejected-overlap.*= and =compare-rejected-overlap.*= and excluded
from qualification. No timing gate, work budget or sample count was relaxed,
and no admitted result was rerun to select a better number.

The final production and focused Unit Test source policy reports pass with
zero findings. Native tests require process success, every =CASE-OK= and
=MODULE-OK=, =HARNESS-OK= and final =OK=; the harness exit alone is insufficient.
